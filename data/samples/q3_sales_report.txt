Executive summary
Total revenue for the third quarter reached 4.8 million riyals, an increase of 9 percent compared with the second quarter. Wholesale orders grew faster than retail, and the online store delivered its best quarter since launch. Gross margin improved to 41 percent because green coffee prices stabilised and the roasting team reduced waste.

Regional results
The Central region contributed 2.1 million riyals, the Western region 1.6 million riyals, and the Eastern region 1.1 million riyals. The Eastern region grew the fastest at 14 percent, helped by three new cafe customers. The Western region was flat because two large accounts delayed their orders until October.

Products
Single-origin beans made up 38 percent of sales, house blends 44 percent, and ready-to-drink bottles 18 percent. Bottled cold brew doubled its volume after the August promotion. Capsule sales declined by 6 percent and the team recommends retiring the two slowest capsule flavours.

Risks and next steps
Shipping delays remain the main risk for the Western region. The team recommends adding a second courier for wholesale deliveries and setting a fourth-quarter revenue target of 5.2 million riyals. A customer survey in November will test demand for a subscription box.
